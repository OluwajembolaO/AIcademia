Student Performance Across Subjects

Changes in average scores and scores at selected percentiles, by subject and grade

The Problem

The data reveals a significant decline in student performance in mathematics and reading across grades 4 and 8 from 2019 to 2022. Scores and percentile performance have dropped across the board, except for the 90th percentile in 4th-grade reading, which remained stable. This downward trend is alarming, indicating that students are struggling more, potentially widening educational gaps and reducing overall academic preparedness. Urgent intervention is needed to address these declines and support students effectively.

Lack Of Resorces

Many students and schools struggle with a lack of resources, such as up-to-date technology, textbooks, and funding, which hinders their ability to provide a quality education.

Unequal Education Access

Unequal access to education remains a significant issue, with disparities in funding, resources, and opportunities creating a gap between students from different socioeconomic backgrounds.

School Completion

Students from low-income backgrounds are less likely to complete college due to financial challenges and limited access to support resources.

Our Mission

Equal Education For All!

AIcademia is dedicated to promoting educational equity by dismantling barriers and providing every student with access to quality learning opportunities. Our mission is to empower learners everywhere, regardless of their background or circumstances, through a wide array of educational resources and tools.
